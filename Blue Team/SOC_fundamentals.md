# SOC Fundamentals

## Overview
- Introduces the purpose and structure of a Security Operations Center.
- Covers the three pillars of an effective SOC and the roles within the team.

## Key Concepts
- A SOC (Security Operations Center) is a 24/7 dedicated team that monitors an organisation's network and systems to detect and respond to threats.
- They have a primary focus of **Detection** and **Response**.

## Three Pillars of a SOC
- **People** - The team involving analysts and engineers who monitor, investigate, and respond to threats.
- **Process** - The procedures and workflows followed when handling incidents.
- **Technology** - The tools used to detect and respond to threats.

## SOC Analyst Levels
- L1 SOC Analyst - Alert triage and reporting.
- L2 SOC Analyst - Deeper investigation of escalated alerts.
- L3 SOC Analyst - Proactive threat hunting and rule creation.

## Process (The 5 W's)
- Who - Which user or system was involved?
- What - What activity was detected?
- When - What time did it occur?
- Where - Which host or IP was targeted?
- Why - What was the likely intention?

## Technology
- SIEM (Security Information and Event Management) - Collects logs from log sources and generates alerts.
- EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response) - Real-time endpoint visibility and response.
- Firewall - Filters incoming and outgoing network traffic.
- IDS/IPS (Intrusion Detection/Prevention System) - Detects/Prevents suspicious network activity.
- SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response) - Automates repetitive response tasks.

## Relevance
- Provides an understanding of how real SOC teams operate and how alerts are triaged using SIEM tools.
