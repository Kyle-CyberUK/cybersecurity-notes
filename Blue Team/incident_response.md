# Incident Response

## Overview
- Covers how security teams detect alerts, contain threats, and recover from cyber incidents.
- It introduces incident types, severity levels, the **SANS/NIST** IR frameworks, and key tools.

## Key Concepts
- **Events** are logs generated by processes running on a device.
- When an event or group of events points to harmful activity, an alert is triggered.
- **True Positive** - Alert correctly identifies a real threat.
- **False Positive** - Alert generated as 'dangerous' but the activity is harmless.

## Types of Security Incidents
- Malware Infection - Malicious software executed on a system.
- Security Breach - Unauthorised access to systems or data.
- Data Leak - Sensitive data exposed or exfiltrated.
- Insider Attack - Threat originating from within the organisation.
- Denial of Service - Attack aimed at disrupting availability of systems or applications.

## IR Frameworks
- **SANS** - 6 Phases (PICERL):
  - Preparation - Build resources and defences before an incident.
  - Identification - Detect suspicious behaviour indicating an incident.
  - Containment - Isolate affected systems to limit spread.
  - Eradication - Remove the threat from the environment.
  - Recovery - Restore affected systems from backup or rebuild.
  - Lessons Learned - Document gaps and improve future responses.
- **NIST** has 4 phases, but follows a very similar structure - combines phases 3, 4, and 5 together.

## IR Tools & Techniques
- SIEM - Centralises logs and correlates them to identify incidents.
- AV - Detects known malicious programs on a system.
- EDR - Endpoint protection deployed on systems - can detect, contain, and eradicate threats.
- Playbooks - Step-by-step guidelines for responding to a specific type of incident.
- Runbooks - Detailed execution steps for specific tasks within an incident.

## Relevance
- Understanding how to triage alerts, classify incidents, and follow a structured response framework (SANS/NIST) is directly applicable to any SOC analyst work and essential on the pathway to more advanced security roles.
